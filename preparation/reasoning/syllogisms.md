# Syllogisms — Day 1

Syllogisms contain statements and conclusions.

We need to check whether the conclusion logically follows.

---

## Important Words

### All

Means the entire group.

Example:

All dogs are animals.

Dogs are inside Animals.

### No

Means there is no overlap.

Example:

No cats are dogs.

Cats and dogs do not overlap.

### Some

Means at least one.

Example:

Some students are programmers.

At least one student is a programmer.

---

# Example 1

Statements:

All dogs are animals.

All animals are living beings.

Conclusion:

All dogs are living beings.

Answer: TRUE

Logic:

Dogs → Animals → Living beings

---

# Example 2

Statements:

All engineers are graduates.

Some graduates are programmers.

Conclusion:

Some engineers are programmers.

Answer: Cannot be concluded.

Why?

We only know:

Engineers → Graduates

Some Graduates → Programmers

We do not know whether those programmers are engineers.

---

# Placement Trick

Do not use outside assumptions.

Only use information given in the statements.
